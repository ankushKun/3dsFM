Features

What it does that others don't

3dsFMlibrary
sdmc:/Music Pocket Park · Miki Matsubara · 1980 Sunshower · Taeko Onuki · 1977 Loose files · 3 songs

Drop folders, get albums

Copy music to sdmc:/Music and that's the library. ID3v1 and ID3v2, Vorbis comments and Opus tags are read; files without tags take their title from the file name and their album and artist from the folders. No database to build, just rescan when you add more.

Top Picks · 28 songs · 19 artists Playlist · mosaic of 4 covers The same picture twice counts once

Mixed folders become playlists

A folder of songs by different artists isn't an album, so it isn't drawn like one. It gets a mosaic of its first four distinct covers, with identical pictures counted once, and every song keeps its own art in the lists and on the top screen.

Not Tomorrow (Long Version) 1:05 of 2:56 · Up next: Promise (Reprise)

The top screen is always the player

Whatever the touch screen is doing, the top screen shows the song that's playing: its cover fogged into the screen, the title set like a game logo, the time, and what's next. Browse the library below without losing sight of it.

Lid closed · still playing Screens off, Wi-Fi awake L and R skip without looking

Plays with the lid shut

Close the lid and the screens go dark while the music carries on, the way the console's own sound app does. L and R still skip songs. Open it and everything is where you left it.

Bars · Wave · Dots · Art only 20 bands, two colors from the cover

A visualizer in the cover's colors

The two main colors are pulled from the album art and drive four styles drawn from the decoded audio. SELECT opens it from anywhere, and the touch screen dims after a while to save battery.

Decoding on the second core 60 fps interface on an Old 3DS Covers decoded in the background

Smooth on an Old 3DS

MP3, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV are decoded in software on the second core, covers are decoded on a background thread and uploaded one per frame, and the interface holds 60 fps. A big cover never stalls a scroll.

Also: a queue with shuffle and repeat, search with the console's keyboard, lists that scroll by drag and flick, long titles that scroll instead of being cut, L and R to skip from any screen, and dark mode by default with the light HOME Menu look a switch away.

Add music

Folders in, albums out

3dsFM reads whatever is under sdmc:/Music on the SD card. Folders become albums, tags fill in the rest, and a rescan in Settings picks up new files.

Which formats?

3dsFM

MP3, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV. Tags come from ID3v1 and ID3v2, Vorbis comments and Opus tags. Files with no tags take their title from the file name and their album and artist from the folders.

Streaming from your own server, saving songs to the SD card and pairing are drawn in the app but switched off until the server exists. Everything above works today, offline.

Questions

Common questions

Notifications
It runs but there's no soundNEW

Homebrew audio needs the console's DSP firmware, which Nintendo doesn't let apps carry. Run DSP1 once: it dumps it to sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc, and 3dsFM finds it from then on. The app tells you when the file is missing.

Which files does it play?NEW

MP3, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV, decoded in software, so an Old 3DS copes too. FLAC and AAC aren't there yet. Convert with ffmpeg if you need to: Opus at 96 kbps sounds very good on the console's speakers and headphones and keeps files small.

My covers don't show upNEW

3dsFM looks for a picture inside each file first, then for cover, folder, front or album images (JPEG or PNG) in the album's folder. Very large pictures are fine; they're scaled on a background thread. If the file's picture is in a format other than JPEG or PNG, the folder image is the fallback.

Why is my folder a "playlist"?NEW

Because its songs have different artists. An album has one cover; a playlist has many, so it's drawn as a mosaic of its first four distinct covers and labelled Playlist. Give the songs the same album artist tag and the same cover if you'd rather it looked like an album.

Does it keep playing with the lid closed?NEW

Yes, when Settings → Keep playing with the lid closed is on, which it is by default. The screens switch off and the music carries on; L and R still skip songs. Pause first if you'd rather the console slept.

Can it stream from somewhere?NEW

Not yet. The screens for pairing a personal 3dsFM server, streaming and saving songs are in the app but disabled until the server ships. Today it's an offline player for the music on your SD card, which is also the part that works on a train.
