# Fonts

`medium24.bcfnt`, `bold24.bcfnt`, `medium12.bcfnt` and `bold12.bcfnt` are M PLUS Rounded 1c (Medium and Bold) by Coji Morishita, converted to the console's BCFNT format with devkitPro's mkbcfnt and limited to Latin, kana and punctuation.
